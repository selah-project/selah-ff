# Deftere Hibraniyanke (Tanakh) — Firo Fulfulde

Firo Deftere Hibraniyanke (Tanakh) e Fulfulde — aaya e aaya,
konngol e konngol; les kala konngol Hibraniyanke, maanaa mun e
Fulfulde.

Ndee ko **jonnde 73** (cappanɗe jeeɗiɗi e tati) e nder Selah.
Repo: `selah-ff`.

## Hibraniyanke no ardii

Ngal firo woodani ko yo ɓadin-maa e binndol Hibraniyanke
asliiwol — wanaa yo lomtino ngol. Ko njanngataa ko Hibraniyanke:
alkulal, konngol, maande. Gorol Fulfulde ngol les mun ko
henorde — wanaa lomtinorde. Ɗo ɗi ceerti — Hibraniyanke on woni
goonga.

## Ko ngal firo wanaa

- **Wanaa lomtinorde Deftere maaɗa.** Jogo deftere maaɗa, njanngaa
  nde, ndu'oɗaa e mayre. Ngal gollal ko sara mun darii — ngal
  holla feewde e asliiwol.
- **Alaa aqiida.** Ko asliiwol tan, konngol e konngol; kala aaya
  ina waawa ƴeewteede.

`NOTES.md` jogii limle tampon on; `manifest.edn` jogii asko ngon.

## Wallu

Peewnugol juumre e wasiyaaji ina njaɓaa — uddit Issue walla neldu
Pull request. E nder yeewtere: haala e teddungal, haala laaɓuka,
ciftora wonde caggal kala innde ina woodi neɗɗo.
